Link it in three steps

  1. Enter your WhatsApp numberWith country code, no + or 0. Example: 94771234567.
  2. Type the pair code in WhatsAppSettings → Linked devices → Link with phone number instead.
  3. Get your Bot KeyThe bot sends it to your WhatsApp. You need it, plus an OTP, to log in to the dashboard.
